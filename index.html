AI agents I built for my team

Writing blog and social posts and tuning ad copy took up a large share of the team's time.

I built AI agents that turn podcast recordings into articles and campaigns, draft and test blog, social and ad copy, and personalize outreach to target accounts. The team learned to use them, and now finds its own uses for AI.

That time goes to strategy, launches and customers now.

Turning HBR research into a campaign for banks

Core system modernization is a dense topic, and bank executives don't have much time. They needed a quick way to see why it mattered and where we fit in.

We sponsored a research program with Harvard Business Review Analytic Services. From the findings we built an infographic, a 14-page whitepaper, a webinar and an analyst report, covering every stage of the buying process.

Every piece, from first awareness to the executive conversation, had Harvard Business Review's name on the findings.

Choose Your Own Adventure, a magazine series

Our buyers care about digital transformation and marketing automation. Most of what's written about them is hard to get through.

I created "Choose Your Own Adventure," a magazine with a retro design, a narrative format and real client stories. I wanted enterprise topics to read like something people want to pick up.

Two issues are out, one on digital transformation and one on marketing automation.

When Finance pulled our fourth-quarter budget

One year Finance pulled our remaining fourth-quarter budget while we were behind on our annual target. Half the team moved to direct support on active deals. The other half focused on organic growth through guest-speaker webinars. We met the annual target without any paid media.

Leading with a security certification in Germany

We built customer profiles country by country. German buyers cared about a government security certification, BSI Grundschutz, so we put it at the center of our message there. It helped us close and renew accounts. North American buyers didn't weigh it, so we left it out.

What others say

Approach

Prove every claim

We back each claim with a customer story, analyst research or test results. Banks and insurers check before they commit, and proof earns their trust early in a long sales cycle.

Teach instead of mandate

Every new tool starts with a demonstration on the task people dislike most. Once people see it work, they find their own uses.

Stay close to the start and finish

Every major project gets direction and context at the start, and review and coaching at the end. In between, the team owns the work and makes the calls. Over five years the team grew from 4 people to 16, and 9 of them were my hires.
